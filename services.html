What we offer

Website services for small businesses.

Choose a simple website structure that suits your business today. We will discuss the details before work begins.

▤

Single-page website

A one-page website that brings your business information together in one place.

  • Business name and introduction
  • Photos and service information
  • Contact details
  • Mobile-friendly layout
  • WhatsApp contact button, when a business number is supplied
▦

Multipage website

A website with separate pages to organise your business information.

  • Up to four pages
  • Home, About, Services and Contact structure
  • Mobile-friendly layout
  • Location and map section if details are supplied
  • Google Sheets integration for agreed stock or information updates
▥

Google Sheets data connection

For suitable projects, a website can display or use information stored in a Google Sheet.

  • Suitable for simple stock lists
  • Agreed columns and data structure
  • Scope confirmed before setup
  • Not a full custom backend or advanced admin system
Our current focus

Honest, achievable solutions.

WebWinkel currently focuses on HTML, CSS and JavaScript websites hosted using GitHub Pages. Google Sheets can be used for suitable data features.

Advanced admin dashboards and full custom backend systems are not currently listed as services. As skills develop, the service offering may expand.
