Hello, I'm the person behind WebWinkel.

WebWinkel was founded in South Africa in 2026. I started this business because I want to build something of my own, grow my skills and help small businesses improve the way they present themselves online.

My focus is on creating single-page and multipage websites using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I can also work with Google Sheets for suitable website data needs.

As the business grows, I want to keep learning, improve my services and build lasting relationships with clients.
